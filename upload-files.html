Files with or without Bleed can both be right.

A common mistake with files being supplied is a lack of crop marks or bleed. What do we mean? Bleed is the area around the project that is printed and then trimmed off. We prefer if your document has a 1/8″ bleed. If there is no bleed – no problem, just make sure your text/images are away from the edge of the document. And, try to avoid borders. If a border is too close to the cut-line, the print may result in a slightly off-centred cut. See the diagram.

PDF (Portable Document Format) is an industry standard in print. Along with CorelDraw, Adobe Illustrator, InDesign and a few others. When exporting from your program to PDF please use Press Quality setting (or similar high resolution setting) and turn on trim marks and put 1/8″ of bleed on the job. This is so we can cut your job to size and avoid any white around the edge. All files supplied should be in CMYK colour space and have fonts converted to outlines or curves to avoid issues.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about sending files and visiting the shop.

How do I send my files?

Use WeTransfer. Click the Upload Files button above, upload your files and send them to andrewscopy@gmail.com. You can also email us directly.

How large a file can I send?

WeTransfer lets you send up to 2 GB for free, including whole folders and entire projects.

Can I email files to be printed?

Yes, we can print from email. See our Basic Service Price List for details.

What file types do you accept?

PDF is an industry standard in print, along with CorelDraw, Adobe Illustrator, InDesign and a few others. When exporting to PDF, please use the Press Quality setting (or a similar high resolution setting).

Do my files need bleed?

We prefer a 1/8″ bleed with trim marks turned on. If there is no bleed, no problem – just keep your text and images away from the edge of the document and try to avoid borders.

What colour mode should my files be in?

All files supplied should be in CMYK colour space and have fonts converted to outlines or curves to avoid issues.

Can you help if I don’t have a design or logo?

Yes. Have an idea for a flyer, invitation, business card, ad layout, ticket, wedding invite, logo, sign or custom stationery but don’t know how to make it look good? Let us do the layout and design work for you. Don’t have a logo? We can create one for you.

How wide can you print?

We copy and print black and white up to 36″ wide, and colour up to 24″ wide for architectural drawings and posters. We can also scan large documents to TIFF or PDF.

What paper and card stock do you carry?

Pastel papers, bright papers, resume quality paper, cardstock and glossy cardstock.

Do you offer laminating and binding?

Yes. We laminate wallet, letter, legal and ledger sizes, and offer roll lamination up to 24″ wide for posters, maps and other large documents. We also bind letter and legal size documents with a choice of covers.

Do you offer folding, collating and stapling?

Yes. We offer folding, hand collating, and stapling by hand or by machine.

Can you number tickets?

Yes. Need it numbered? No problem and no extra cost.

Do you offer scanning?

Yes. We can scan a document and email it to you or transfer it to your USB drive. We also offer photo scanning.

What else can you print?

Photo and logo shirts, custom mousepads, ceramic photo mugs and calendars. We also carry stationery and office supplies. If you don’t see it listed, call us and we’ll be happy to provide a quote.
